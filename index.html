About Ethereal Agro

Ethereal Agro is based in Jogingumpa, Maharashtra, and offers organic agro products to local farmers. For over 17 years, we have worked hand-in-hand with farmers to champion sustainable, chemical-free agriculture. Our passion lies in nurturing soil health, restoring natural ecosystems, and empowering farming communities with effective organic solutions. We care deeply about delivering wholesome, eco-conscious products that protect our land for future generations while supporting the dedicated hands that cultivate it.

What sets our products apart is complete transparency from source to shelf. Sourced directly from trusted organic farms and crafted using traditional, eco-friendly extraction and processing methods, every item preserves its natural purity and nutrient potency. We ensure zero synthetic additives, toxic chemicals, or fillers—just honest, uncompromised quality harvested straight from nature.
